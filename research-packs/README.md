# Experimental research specifications

**UNEXECUTED RESEARCH PROPOSALS.** These specifications identify questions,
candidate representations, conventional comparisons and outcomes that would
reject the proposed benefit. Their synthetic mathematics is distinguished
from data-driven prediction. No new biological, clinical or performance
result is reported.

The main manuscript supplies the core definitions and written mathematical
bridges. Each pack restates the model and endpoint needed to understand its
own proposal. A mathematical review of a specification does not establish
that an eventual implementation is correct or that its scientific hypothesis
is true.

| Pack | Concrete question | Current state |
|---|---|---|

| [BRCA1 cellular function](brca1-function/README.md) | Can a receiver-conditioned representation retain supplied assay information and earn a useful comparison with ordinary feature selection? | Finite score audit and evaluation-contract corrections reviewed; source-column mapping, actual predictors and evaluation unexecuted. This is an assay question, not a treatment or individual-risk result. |
| [Immune evidence and mechanisms](immune-mechanisms/README.md) | Can complete joint constraints preserve unresolved alternatives and identify informative next questions within a supplied catalogue? | Literature-level evidence map and corrected finite inference specification reviewed; no reusable joint quantitative benchmark or scientific execution. |

For every proposed comparison, retain the same endpoint, available inputs,
prediction population, missingness rules and total cost accounting for the
candidate and its baseline. Preserve selection failures and inconclusive
outcomes. A score on one outcome space cannot be compared directly with a
score on a different space. A finite exact audit cannot be substituted for
a held-out empirical result.

Source links identify acquisition leads and scholarship. They do not supply
local data bytes or authorize redistribution. No source dataset, trained
weights, individual record or private corpus material is bundled here.
Each pack names its remaining input and execution obligations so work can
continue from a concrete, honest starting point.
