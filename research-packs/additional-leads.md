# Additional research leads

**Supporting material; no new scientific result.** The main book develops
folding dynamics and BRCA1 cellular function because they exercise its two
mathematical tests in detail. The following directions remain accessible
here without adding two more abbreviated proposals to that reading sequence.

## Immune evidence and mechanisms

The [existing immune specification](immune-mechanisms/README.md) retains
published mechanism evidence, joint compatibility constraints and unresolved
alternatives. Its current deliverable is an evidence map. A predictive
comparison still needs a suitable independent joint dataset; combining
separate publications does not create that dataset. No diagnostic or
treatment claim is reported.

## Equilibrium stability: further reading

The original reading edition pointed to the cDNA-display proteolysis study
of [Tsuboyama and colleagues (2023)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06328-6).
That published-study pointer is retained here as further reading. There is
no reviewed stability research specification, executable stability tool or
new stability result in this release. A static lattice contact score must
not be relabeled as an experimental free-energy measurement. Structure,
thermodynamic stability, cellular abundance and folding dynamics remain
different questions.
