# A sufficient variance condition for joining quantum patches

12 September 2026. This is a conditional lemma for the next quantum
obligation. Its implication is proved below; the required mixing and local
constants have not been established uniformly for Yang--Mills graph families.
It builds on the source quantum model and ground-state transform in
[GAP_BRIDGE.md](GAP_BRIDGE.md), without changing that model's claim ceiling.

## Contract

Fix one finite connected graph `G` with a nonconstant physical sector, the
compact source `Q_G=SU(2)^(E_G)`, product Haar probability `mu_G`, full vertex
gauge invariance, and

```text
H_G = -(alpha hbar^2/2) sum_(e,k) D_(e,k)^2 + V_G,
alpha>0, hbar>0.
```

Assume the specified smooth real gauge-invariant potential and source
operator domain give the unique normalized positive physical ground state
`psi_0`. Write `nu_G=psi_0^2 mu_G` and `gamma_G` for the gap above its energy.
The derivatives and metric retain the `i sigma_k`, `-Tr(XY)/2` normalization.
Wavefunctions are equal almost everywhere. A physical test function `f` is
gauge invariant; its variance is

```text
Var_nu(f)=integral |f-integral f dnu|^2 dnu.
```

It suffices to state the inequalities below on a dense smooth physical form
core and extend them by form-norm closure. Equivalently, assume them directly
for the physical functions in the inherited ground-state form domain.

Supply a finite family of nonempty edge subsets (patch occurrences) `B`.
For each patch let `F_(B^c)` be the sigma algebra generated by the link
variables on edges outside `B`, and define the integrated conditional variance

```text
v_B(f)=E_nu[Var_nu(f | F_(B^c))]
      =E_nu[|f-E_nu(f | F_(B^c))|^2].                    (G1)
```

These are conditional laws under the same full vacuum measure. They need
not be product measures, independent patches, or standalone physical
patch states. Gauge-invariant `f` is the only function class required here;
no assertion for arbitrary gauge-dependent local functions is needed.

The supplied ports for the lemma are positive constants `C_mix,C_loc` and
an integer `m>=1` satisfying all three conditions:

```text
Var_nu(f) <= C_mix sum_B v_B(f),                         (G2)

v_B(f) <= C_loc integral sum_(e in B,k) |D_(e,k) f|^2 dnu
    for every patch B,                                 (G3)

#{B : e in B} <= m for every edge occurrence e.         (G4)
```

Condition (G3) is the integrated conditional inequality. An exterior-almost-
everywhere conditional inequality with the same constant would imply it,
but that stronger hypothesis is unnecessary. Repeated patches, if allowed
in the family, count with their multiplicity in (G2) and (G4). Shared links
remain the same edge occurrence throughout; they are never copied into
independent variables.

## Lemma and proof

Under (G2)--(G4),

```text
gamma_G >= alpha hbar^2 / (2 C_mix C_loc m).             (G5)
```

Indeed, applying the two variance estimates and then counting each edge's
actual multiplicity gives

```text
Var_nu(f)
 <= C_mix C_loc sum_B integral sum_(e in B,k) |D_(e,k) f|^2 dnu
 <= C_mix C_loc m integral sum_(e,k) |D_(e,k) f|^2 dnu.
```

The source ground-state transform identifies the physical gap with
`(alpha hbar^2/2)` times the infimum of the last Dirichlet integral divided
by `Var_nu(f)`, over nonconstant physical form-domain functions. This proves
(G5). Mean zero under `nu_G` is exactly orthogonality of `psi_0 f` to the
quantum ground state. Thus the landing concerns the original physical
spectrum, with its source operator domain.

## A distinguishing correlation control

Separate local conditional estimates cannot supply (G2) with a uniform
constant. Consider the explicitly different finite carrier `(x,y) in
{-1,+1}^2`, with `0<epsilon<=1/2`, probability `(1-epsilon)/2` at each
same-sign pair, and `epsilon/2` at each opposite-sign pair. Use singleton
patches `{x}` and `{y}`, so their overlap multiplicity is one. For `f=x+y`,

```text
E f=0,                 Var(f)=4(1-epsilon),
E[x|y]=(1-2epsilon)y,  Var(x|y)=4epsilon(1-epsilon),
v_{x}(f)=v_{y}(f)=4epsilon(1-epsilon).
```

Therefore the exact ratio for this one test function is

```text
Var(f)/(v_{x}(f)+v_{y}(f))=1/(2epsilon).                 (G6)
```

Any constant valid in (G2) for this carrier must be at least `1/(2epsilon)`.
For clarity, its local inequalities can nevertheless have a uniform
constant. Define the discrete flip derivative
`d_x h(x,y)=h(x,y)-h(-x,y)`, and similarly for `y`. Conditional on `y`, a
two-point variance is `epsilon(1-epsilon)` times the squared difference
of its two values. Hence, for every function `h`,

```text
v_{x}(h)<= (1/4) E|d_x h|^2,
v_{y}(h)<= (1/4) E|d_y h|^2.
```

Thus bounded local constants and fixed overlap coexist with an unbounded
required mixing constant. This control refutes that implication; it does
not by itself assert failure of every other global Poincare estimate or
assign this discrete distribution to an `SU(2)` vacuum.

## What remains open

For a family indexed by volume and lattice spacing, all constants in
(G2)--(G4) refer to the actual family of joint vacuum measures and may vary
with those indices. The two-square gap does not establish (G2) or the
conditional estimates (G3) on a larger graph. Their derivation, with useful
uniform constants, is the missing mathematical port.

A physical-unit lower bound requires the whole ratio in (G5) to have one
positive lower bound independent of volume and spacing along the declared
limit. If an additional conversion factor
`s(a)>0` multiplies reference energies, the relevant lower bound is
`s(a) alpha(a) hbar^2/[2 C_mix(a) C_loc(a) m(a)]`; if `alpha` already has
physical energy units, take `s=1`. Separate bounds on constants without
their energy scaling do not settle this condition. Continuum construction
and preservation of the required quantum observables remain further
obligations even if such a uniform estimate is obtained.

Evidence grade: written sufficient-condition proof and exact four-state
counterexample calculation. No new computation or solved uniformity claim
is supplied. The constants and the continuum gap remain **OPEN**.
