# Primary-source starting points — not new evidence for YM2

Checked on 12 September 2026 when preparing this research assignment. This handoff verified the landing pages/abstracts below, not their complete technical proofs. The agent must inspect the relevant equations before adopting a model. Full third-party works are not copied into this package.

1. H.-P. Pavel, *SU(2) Yang-Mills quantum mechanics of spatially constant fields*, Phys. Lett. B 648 (2007), 97–106, arXiv:hep-th/0701283. https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0701283 . The abstract confirms an established spatially constant SU(2) model and gauge reduction. Its quantum variational results are not imported into the present classical investigation and do not establish the requested 3+1D continuum gap.
2. S. A. Gogilidze, A. M. Khvedelidze, D. M. Mladenov, H.-P. Pavel, *Hamiltonian reduction of SU(2) Dirac-Yang-Mills mechanics*, Phys. Rev. D 57 (1998), 7488–7500, arXiv:hep-th/9707136. https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9707136 . Primary lead for constrained homogeneous gauge mechanics. It includes fermions; justify a pure-gauge restriction instead of silently borrowing a different model.
3. David Tong, *Gauge Theory*, chapter 2 (Yang–Mills) and chapter 4 (lattice gauge theory), official teaching page: https://davidtong.org/teaching/gauge-theory/ . The Cambridge page https://www.damtp.cam.ac.uk/user/tong/gaugetheory.html currently redirects there. Use only the sections needed for the chosen model; do not turn this into a long literature sweep.
4. The previously consulted Maxwell and Jaffe–Witten references remain in sources/c2/yang_mills/PRIMARY_REFERENCES.md. They support the earlier model/target definitions, not the new interacting closure claim.
