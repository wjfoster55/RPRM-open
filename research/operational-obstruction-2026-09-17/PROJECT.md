# Exact operational obstruction fibers

17 September 2026. This is a new research cut, not a Manifesto edit, not a
catch-up rewrite, and not a continuation of last night's `T(X,P)` order theorem.

## The pick

**Complete O05 obstruction fibers for a proposed summary**, including the
pairs that FIVE.future cannot see.

Overnight counted how many *unknown* partial maps a partition survives, and
proved that enabledness-on-blocks is the extra that makes the extremal law a
theorem. That count is closed. The dual instrument was still missing: given a
*declared* machine and a proposed summary `C`, return the **complete**
classified obstruction of `C` against O05, not the first REJECT witness, and
not a distinguishing word that can be NONE while the update still fails.

That is independent-math Rank 1 (exact abstraction repair + distinguishing
witnesses) applied to a gap that is already named in `docs/operations.md`:
FIVE.future cannot expose a representational update failure that leaves every
admitted future answer equal; `CHECK.operational` can. The live code did the
second job only as a first-witness gate in `rprm/core.py`. First-witness REJECT
is the same kind of incomplete fiber as successor-only counting: it can hide
the rest of the obstruction.

## Why this is the best next *new science* cut

Last night's lesson, used rather than repeated:

- **Source-level yield.** A checkable oracle plus a complete census on a
  declared family, not a dialect note.
- **No fitted repair.** The repair is the unique O09 least stable refinement
  already in `rprm/futures.py`. This module only asks whether `C` is already
  that object, and if not, returns every failing pair classified by clause.
- **Hostile cases first.** The Manifesto three-state example; two simultaneous
  observation failures against core's first witness; O08R question-repair that
  is still not operational; n=2 and one-block controls that cannot ghost-fold;
  kernel equal-support kept outside the PARTIAL claim.
- **Published objects.** O05 / O05F / O08R / O09, Moore-style refinement,
  FIVE.future. No private prestige/Dark-World vocabulary.

The first closed fiber is the trichotomy of merged pairs
(observation / enabledness / successor / pass), the ghost-fold subfiber
(future-sufficient `C` that still fails O05), and an exhaustive census of
every partition of every machine in the existing 845-machine futures family.

**Null, declared before the census:** those ghost folds are only the Manifesto
three-state example and its state relabelings. **The null is false.** The
family contains MANY(72) ghost folds, of which 12 are that sink/jump pattern
and 60 are other total constant-observation maps. The complete 72 are in
`CENSUS.json`. A 2×2 of `(sink|return)×(self|partner)` names all 72: 12+12+24+24,
unclassified NONE. The one-type null for the leftover 60 is false; they
are 12 `sink_partner`, 24 `return_self`, and 24 `return_partner`. See
[THEORY.md](THEORY.md) §7.

**Hostile next cut, declared before looking:** that 2×2 still names every
ghost fold on the four-state one-action family and the three-state
two-action family (same constructor as the 845). **The null is false.**
The families contain MANY(9360) ghost folds: 5424 keep a 2×2 name and 3936
do not. Five occupied residue types (`split`, `escape`, `crowd`, `multi`,
`mixed`) are typed definitions, not leftover buckets; `partial_land` and
`wide_landing` occupy 0 among ghosts; unclassified NONE. Match null against
those definitions: **true.** The local unique-pair slice is also not a 2×2:
7056 unique-pair ghosts, of which 1632 are `split`/`escape`/`mixed`. The
replacement is the a priori lift vocabulary, not a fifth binary bit. See
[THEORY.md](THEORY.md) §9–§10 and [LIFT_CENSUS.json](LIFT_CENSUS.json).
