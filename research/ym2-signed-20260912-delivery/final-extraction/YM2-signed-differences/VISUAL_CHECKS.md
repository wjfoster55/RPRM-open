# Figure verification

`correction_supports.png` was generated at 2080 by 1440 pixels with the
already installed matplotlib and visually inspected. The same drawing is
also exported as editable `correction_supports.svg`.

The open support is exactly `{a,l,c}` with active degree-one endpoints B
and E. The closed comparison adds the shared edge s. The four lower
supports are `{a,l,c,s}`, `{b,r,d,s}`, the six-edge outer loop, and all seven
edges, with the corresponding weights `d^4,d^4,d^6,d^7`.

Titles, node labels, equations and explanatory captions were legible and
unclipped in the inspected rendering. The display explicitly identifies
these as heat-kernel correction supports rather than field trajectories.
No graphical shape is being offered as a proof in place of the written
gauge cancellation and support-coverage arguments.

research checkers do not require matplotlib or regenerate media.
