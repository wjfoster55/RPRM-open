# BSD arithmetic resumption: exact doubling classes and a compatible descent tower

**12 September 2026.** New worked calculation in this conversation, using standard
2-descent. This is not a mathematical-priority claim, a new BSD theorem, a paper,
or an agent dispatch. The Lind–Reichardt review005 and old AD pilot stay closed.

## 0. Source reconciliation and one selected question

Read the supplied `BSD_CHAT_UPDATE.md` and `CURRENT_STATE.md` and the accepted
C1/AD1 documents. C1 is a quotient/restriction algebra with retained reconstruction
recipes, not literal low-degree representatives closed under full multiplication.
AD1 has an explicit reuse/transport contract and ties ordinary typed caching;
no general performance advantage is imported. Prestige One retains usable
relational access and construction; this arithmetic realization does not replace
that broader meaning. Fiving, FIVE_IS_SAFE, and FIVE.future are not silently
identified with arithmetic modulo five or with this curve's subscript.

The unfinished arithmetic source is the original `RESEARCH_NOTE.md`, sections 2–4:
actual rational-point classes versus Selmer classes, and the maps between descent
levels. The selected question is:

> Can we give the rational part of a descent tower explicit compatible coordinates,
> with actual witnesses and a reconstruction route, rather than assuming every
> locally compatible class is global or that a coarse class identifies its source?

We answer this on one positive-rank example, E: y^2=x^3-25x. This is a fresh
classical calculation, not another audit of the frozen obstruction curve.

## 1. Curve, group, squareclasses, and standard inputs

Let

    E/Q: y^2 = x(x-5)(x+5),       G=E(Q).

O is the projective identity. The discriminant is 1,000,000=2^6*5^6, so E is
nonsingular. The four rational points killed by doubling are

    K=G[2]={O, T0=(0,0), Tplus=(5,0), Tminus=(-5,0)}.

All additions, subtractions, doubles, and halves below use the elliptic-curve group
law. Reducing modulo 2G is NOT coordinate reduction modulo 2.

For a nonzero rational number a, [a] is its class in Q*/Q*^2. A class is represented
by a signed squarefree integer. For a point R away from O and the three roots, put

    delta(R)=([x(R)],[x(R)-5],[x(R)+5]).

The product is the trivial squareclass, since y(R)^2=x(R)(x(R)-5)(x(R)+5).
At O the signature is (1,1,1). At Ti=(ei,0), the zero entry is replaced by
(ei-ej)(ei-ek); this retains the usual Kummer map rather than treating zero as
an element of the multiplicative squareclass group.

**Standard theorem dependencies:** delta is a homomorphism with kernel 2G;
H^1(Q,E[2]) is represented by the squareclass triples with square product; the
2-Selmer group is the subgroup whose restrictions belong to the local Kummer
images at every place; and

    0 -> G/2G -> Sel_2(E/Q) -> Sha(E/Q)[2] -> 0.

These are not re-proved by finite tests. See Milne, IV §3 Remark 3.7, printed
p.113 (PDF p.121), and Stoll §1.1. Mordell–Weil finite generation will be used to
turn the exact quotient count into a rank.

## 2. Eight explicit rational classes: a lower bound

Take P=(-4,6), since 36=-64+100. Let H be the subgroup of squareclass triples
generated by delta(P), delta(T0), and delta(Tplus). Their eight distinct products
have the following exact rational representatives.

| Class representative R | Rational coordinates | delta(R) |
|---|---|---|
| O | O | (1,1,1) |
| T0 | (0,0) | (-1,-5,5) |
| Tplus | (5,0) | (5,2,10) |
| Tminus | (-5,0) | (-5,-10,2) |
| P | (-4,6) | (-1,-1,1) |
| P+T0 | (25/4,75/8) | (1,5,5) |
| P+Tplus | (-5/9,-100/27) | (-5,-2,10) |
| P+Tminus | (45,-300) | (5,10,2) |

Thus H has eight elements and

    H subset delta(G) subset Sel_2(E/Q).

No rank or database label is used to obtain this lower bound. The point identities
and the signatures can be verified directly using fractions.

## 3. Why only 32 initial candidates need consideration

This restriction is valid for Selmer elements, not just for rational point
signatures. Let q be an odd prime other than 5. Every local Kummer signature has
even q-valuation in each coordinate:

- If v_q(x)<0, all three factors x,x-5,x+5 have the same valuation m. Their
  product is a square, so 3m is even, and m is even.
- If v_q(x)>=0, the factors are integral. Their pairwise differences are 5 or
  10, which are q-units. At most one factor has positive valuation; the product
  being a square makes that valuation even.
- The explicitly defined O and two-torsion signatures satisfy the same condition.

Selmer membership imposes these local conditions at every q. Therefore the
rational squareclass representatives have support only at 2 and 5 and a sign:

    D={-10,-5,-2,-1,1,2,5,10}.

The product condition determines d3 from d1*d2. This gives 64 triples. The real
Kummer image has signs (+++) or (--+), including the special-point conventions.
Consequently, d1 and d2 have the same sign and d3 is positive. The resulting
32-element group is denoted V. Thus

    H subset Sel_2(E/Q) subset V.

No conclusion that a surviving finite congruence is locally soluble is being used.

## 4. Three local obstructions finish the global class count

For d=(d1,d2,d3), local realizability requires a nonzero projective solution of

    d1*u1^2 - d2*u2^2 = 5*t^2,
    d3*u3^2 - d1*u1^2 = 5*t^2.                       (1)

For ordinary non-torsion points this follows by putting x-ei=di*(ui/t)^2.
The projective equations include the O and torsion cases. Product(d_i) is a square,
so these are the usual full-2-descent covering equations.

A Q_2 solution can be scaled into Z_2^4 with at least one odd coordinate. Therefore
absence of a primitive solution modulo 4 or 8 proves absence of a Q_2 solution.

There is a short coset proof of completeness. With componentwise squareclass
multiplication, the four cosets of H in V have representatives

    (1,1,1),       A=(2,2,1),       B=(1,2,2),       AB=(2,1,2).

They are disjoint and cover V. For example, H has odd first components; A and AB
have even first components, B is not in H, and A*B=AB. The code also enumerates
these four blocks exactly. Since Sel_2 is a group containing H, finding any Selmer
element in one of the three nontrivial cosets would put its representative in
Sel_2. It suffices to exclude A, B, and AB.

### A=(2,2,1): contradiction modulo 4

Equations (1) become

    2u1^2-2u2^2=5t^2,      u3^2-2u1^2=5t^2.

The first equation modulo 2 makes t even. Modulo 4 the second then reads
u3^2=2u1^2. Since square residues modulo 4 are 0 and 1, both u1 and u3 must be
even. The first equation now makes u2 even. All coordinates are even,
contradicting primitivity.

### AB=(2,1,2): contradiction modulo 4

Now

    2u1^2-u2^2=5t^2,      2u3^2-2u1^2=5t^2.

The second equation makes t even. The first modulo 4 makes u1,u2 even; the second
then makes u3 even. Again there is no primitive solution.

### B=(1,2,2): contradiction modulo 8

Now

    u1^2-2u2^2=5t^2,      2u3^2-u1^2=5t^2.

If t is even, reduction modulo 2 and then modulo 4 makes u1,u2,u3 all even.
If t is odd, u1 is odd, so modulo 8 the first equation says

    1-2u2^2=5,     hence u2^2=2 (mod 4),

which is impossible. Thus B also has no Q_2 realization.

All nontrivial cosets are excluded. We have proved

    Sel_2(E/Q)=delta(G)=H,       |G/2G|=8,       Sha(E/Q)[2]=0.       (2)

The full primitive residue enumeration independently gives the same upper bound:
32 real-admissible triples; 16 survive modulo 4; exactly eight survive modulo 8;
those eight are precisely H. The proof does not infer Q_2-solubility from survival:
the rational points in §2 already establish global, and hence all-local,
realization for every surviving class.

## 5. The global rank and the 2-primary obstruction

By Mordell–Weil, G is Z^r plus a finite torsion group T. For finite T,
|T/2T|=|T[2]| by the kernel/image count. Here |G[2]|=4, and therefore

    |G/2G|=2^r*4.

Combining with (2) gives 8=2^r*4, so

    rank E(Q)=1.

Equation (2) also rules out every nonzero element of Sha killed by a power of 2:
any element of order 2^k would give nonzero order-2 torsion after multiplication
by 2^(k-1). Hence

    Sha(E/Q)[2^n]=0 for every n>=1.                    (3)

This is not a conclusion about odd-primary Sha or its total order.

None of the three nonzero T in G[2] has trivial delta, so none lies in 2G.
There can therefore be no rational point of order 4, or of higher 2-power order.
The 2-primary part of the rational torsion group is exactly K=(Z/2)^2.

P is not congruent to any rational torsion point modulo 2G. With rank one, its
coefficient relative to a free generator is odd. We have NOT proved that this
odd coefficient is 1. Full integral generator saturation at odd primes is not
needed for any of the conclusions below and was not performed.

## 6. Compatible coordinates on the entire 2-power Selmer tower

At each level, the standard exact sequence is

    0 -> G/2^n G -> Sel_{2^n}(E/Q) -> Sha(E/Q)[2^n] -> 0.

By (3), its left map is an isomorphism. We can give explicit quotient coordinates:

    Psi_n: (Z/2^n) x K -> G/2^n G,
    Psi_n(m,T)=[mP+T]_(2^n).

The odd free coefficient of P is invertible modulo every 2^n. Odd-order torsion
contributes nothing to this quotient, and K injects because there is no higher
2-primary torsion. Thus Psi_n is a group isomorphism. In particular,

    Sel_{2^n}(E/Q) ~= Z/2^n x (Z/2)^2,
    |Sel_{2^n}(E/Q)|=2^(n+2).

These are proved group descriptions, not separate brute-force enumerations of
covering equations for every n.

The transition induced by E[2^(n+1)] -> E[2^n], multiplication by 2, corresponds
on rational-point quotients to reduction:

    (m mod 2^(n+1), T) -> (m mod 2^n, T).

Every class has two lifts: (m,T) and (m+2^n,T). A class represented by an actual
rational point lifts by keeping that same point. This is NOT the operation of
taking a rational half of the point.

## 7. An executable decoder with an actual reconstruction remainder

For any supplied rational point R, use its signature to choose the unique
(epsilon,T) in {0,1} x K with

    delta(R)=delta(epsilon*P+T).

Then R-epsilon*P-T is a rational double. Take a rational half R1. Repeat this on
R1. After n steps:

    R = mP + T0 + 2^n Rn,          0 <= m < 2^n.       (4)

The later torsion terms disappear because twice every element of K is O. This
algorithm returns the quotient coordinate (m,T0) and, separately, the rational
remainder Rn needed for exact reconstruction. It does not identify the quotient
coordinate with the original rational point.

For S=(x,y) in 2G, S!=O, choose rational square roots

    r1^2=x, r2^2=x-5, r3^2=x+5, r1*r2*r3=-y.

Put a=r1*r2+r1*r3+r2*r3. A rational half is

    (x+a, -y-(r1+r2+r3)*a).

The four allowed sign choices give all four halves. See Bekker–Zarhin §2,
equations (3)–(6). The implementation verifies each returned half independently
with the chord/tangent group law. For S=O, the halves are K and the implementation
chooses O. No root is selected merely because it looks like a positive branch.

Each finite n terminates. We do not claim an infinite descent terminates at O,
or that the quotient representatives constitute full integral generators.
For comparison, 3Z maps surjectively onto every Z/2^n, but 3Z is not all of Z.
Binary quotient coverage does not settle odd-index saturation.

## 8. Worked changed-question example

Direct doubling gives

    Q=2P=(1681/144, -62279/1728).

All three factors used by delta are squares:

    x(Q)=(41/12)^2,
    x(Q)-5=(31/12)^2,
    x(Q)+5=(49/12)^2.

Therefore delta(Q)=(1,1,1)=delta(O). This is correct: Q and O belong to the same
class modulo 2G. It does not mean Q=O.

At the next level, Q is not in 4G. Its four rational halves are the four P+T
points listed in §2, and none has trivial delta. None is itself a rational double.
Equivalently, in the compatible coordinates,

    [Q] mod 2G=(0,O),     [Q] mod 4G=(2,O),
    [O] mod 2G=(0,O),     [O] mod 4G=(0,O).

The coarse label alone cannot recover which higher-level class the source occupied.
Retaining R, the reconstruction remainder, or other adequate arithmetic evidence
resolves the question. Choosing an arbitrary lift is not the same as recovering
the source's lift. Group addition is nevertheless exactly valid within each quotient.

## 9. What this establishes for the unfinished BSD direction

The original unfinished lifting identity was, with G_n=G/2^nG and S_n=Sel_{2^n},

    0 -> G_n -> image(S_(n+k)->S_n) -> 2^k Sha[2^(n+k)] -> 0.

Here we have not assumed the last group vanishes: §4 proves Sha[2]=0, and §5
propagates that result. Thus we obtain an exact, fully rational tower in this
example, including its class coordinates, transition maps, and a finite-level
decoder with readback.

For a different curve with extra Selmer classes, the same conclusion cannot be
copied. One must control the obstruction contribution. A standard next tool is
the Cassels pairing on Sel_2: its kernel is the image of Sel_4, as stated in
Fisher–Schaefer–Stoll §3. No pairing was computed in this attempt.

The BSD target is still the identification

    rank E(Q) = order at s=1 of L(E,s).

We established the arithmetic rank 1 and the absence of 2-primary Sha. We did
not compute L(E,s), prove nonvanishing of its first derivative, establish the
leading-coefficient formula, or determine odd-primary Sha. No measured efficiency
or general advantage is claimed. The RPRM connection is a proved operational
quotient and retained route for stronger queries, not a renamed numerical pattern.

## 10. Actual execution and artifact scope

`e5_descent.py` is a standard-library implementation with exact integers and
fractions. `E5_DESCENT_RECEIPT.json` records:

- all 64 product-square supported triples and the 32 real-admissible count;
- primitive congruence enumeration modulo 2,4,8, cross-checked through weighted
  square-residue sets with parity information;
- the four-coset partition and three excluded representatives;
- eight exact rational points, their distinct signatures, and homogeneous witnesses;
- the Q versus O example and the four halves of Q;
- 256 finite-level decoder cases: R=mP+T, -5<=m<=10, T in K, levels n=1..4,
  checking exact reconstruction (4), not only the quotient labels.

These are newly run exposed development calculations. The finite checks support
the implementation. The universal conclusions additionally depend on the written
arguments and cited standard theorems. No Sage, database ranks, numerical
L-functions, full Sha computation, worker dispatch, or old-audit replay was used.
