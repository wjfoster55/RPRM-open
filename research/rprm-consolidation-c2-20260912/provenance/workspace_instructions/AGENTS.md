# Working with RPRM

Start with README.md and AGENT_HANDBOOK.md; MANIFESTO.md supplies the connected
argument, and docs/core.md and docs/operations.md give more detail. This project
develops a relational framework for mathematical unification: preserve the
source mathematics and make its questions, representations and operations
explicit. Every claim has a stated domain and evidence grade.

For a mathematical task, specify:

1. Carrier, types, equality and admitted context.
2. Supplied ports, missing ports and requested readout.
3. Operation kind, direction and enabledness.
4. Receiver: the observations and continuations to preserve.
5. Inverse, retraction, or complete preimage/completion fiber.
6. Coverage boundary, a distinguishing hostile case, and evidence grade.

Return NONE, ONE(value), or MANY(family) only for a complete solved fiber.
An unfinished search is OPEN; malformed input is an admission error.
A new required type or operation creates a new explicit contract.

Keep equal values distinct from equal occurrences. Keep correlated missing
ports joint. A representation preserves a question exactly when that question
is constant on each representation fiber. An operational quotient also needs
matching enabledness and retained successor behavior.

Separate definitions, written proofs, formal proofs, finite tests,
conjectures and interpretations. A hash binds bytes; it does not prove their
contents. Tool self-tests do not establish their own general soundness.
Preserve counterexamples and corrections alongside surviving results.

Use adjacent, documented modules and the declared verification commands.
Do not treat example observations as an unbounded theorem, a new physical
law, or evidence of an unstated relation between unrelated systems.

Forks may change these conventions under the repository's licenses. State
changed contracts and keep examples reproducible. No maintenance response
from the original release is assumed.
