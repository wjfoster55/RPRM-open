# Source and evidence boundary

The requested basis is the current conversation's completed closeouts, earlier landscape/owner cards, recovered concepts and attached evidence. This packet reorganizes the remaining tasks; it does not quietly upgrade old claims or add a new scientific result.

- `accepted/` carries final C1 and M1 builds/evidence and current acceptance decisions. Their old test commands are not automatically active jobs.
- `sources/bsd/` and `sources/ad/` are exact copies of the review records bundled in the earlier landscape archive. They are adequate for the current scope/claim design, not a complete reconstruction of every earlier arithmetic experiment or every pilot dispatch. Do not pretend missing historical context has been recovered.
- `sources/yang_mills/` contains the old limited calibration. The new model selection/derivation is work assigned to Codex, not a result claimed in this handoff.
- `sources/recovered/` preserves original phrases alongside attributed formalizations and their limits. No finite realization is silently substituted for the whole original idea.
- `history/` keeps earlier routing instructions and the older SAT R1 overlay verbatim. CURRENT_STATE.md explicitly states what supersedes their operational status.
- `source_access/` contains the pinned fluid retrieval plan and a normalized observation of current PR metadata. The full fluid source/result tree is not embedded. It must be inspected from an authorized local or remote source by Codex.

The packaging pass verifies source-copy identities, archive membership, SHA-256 and fresh extraction only. It does not run the C1/SAT mathematical suites, fluid simulations, the AD pilot, or a Yang–Mills model. External field-theory references must be checked by Codex before relying on them for YM1. No new run is represented by a historical receipt.

The deliverable manifest hashes files in this packet, including frozen nested archives. Its self-entry is excluded explicitly. The final packaging-validation record and ZIP digest are outside the ZIP so recording them does not change the artifact being identified.
