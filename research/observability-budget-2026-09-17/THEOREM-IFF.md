# When extra time refines Q

Let `C_S` be a total panel and `T` a total action (or a finite alphabet).
Extra time **weakly refines Q** at horizon `b` iff some word `w` of
length at most `b` splits a present Q-ghost:

`C_S(x)=C_S(y)`, `Q(x)≠Q(y)`, `C_S(T_w x)≠C_S(T_w y)`.

If `C_S ∘ T` is a function of `C_S` (a dynamical homomorphism / a
congruence), no such pair exists at any horizon. The converse is false
as a Q-statement: homomorphism failure may split only Q-constant pairs.

That is the whole claim. It is not a new prefix-closedness theorem about
the shift. Stop padding.

`Y∘shift` is not a function of `Y`. Frozen `N_y_delay` said horizon 1
misses every Q-ghost; the census is **ONE(`N_y_immediate`)** because
`(000)` and `(011)` are a Q-ghost already split by one shift, while
`(000)` and `(001)` wait until horizon 2. Repair of all of `Q=c` still
