## One exact reading of 59, the hinge, and 595

Take `59`, reverse it to `95`, and join the two words at their shared `9`:

```
59 + 95, overlapping the common 9 -> 595
```

Here `+` describes joining words, not integer addition. Define the join
by the longest suffix of the first word matching a prefix of the second,
including complete overlap. For any two decimal digits a,b:

```
F(ab) = aba  when a != b
F(aa) = aa
```

This gives `59 -> 595`, `58 -> 585`, and `55 -> 55`. Every equal-digit
word is fixed; this is not a universal special status for 55. Ordinary
doubling would give 118, and copying 59 twice would give 5959.

With a separately declared `2` tag meaning "two views," the record
`59 | 2 | 595` serializes to `592595`. This is a candidate selected after
seeing the target digits. It is not evidence that the original bound
was generated by the proposed operation, or that 2 universally means
this operation in RPRM.

The carrier is all 100 ordered two-digit words, including `00` and `05`;
equality retains width. Reversal is enabled on every carrier element.
Taking the first two characters of F(w) recovers w. The [digit proof and
tests](agents/DIGIT_SCOUT.md) completely classify its fibers on all 1,100
two- or three-digit target words: 100 singleton fibers and 1,000 empty
fibers. The general intended user grammar remains OPEN. In particular,
joining at exactly one symbol also gives 595 and 585, but gives 555
instead of 55. The proposed test `58 -> 585` cannot distinguish those
two rules; `55 -> 55` versus `55 -> 555` can.

For `7394`, an optional parser `t|a|9|b` can retain tag 7 and pair (3,4).
That is a new positional convention, not a discovered division law.
Reading the pair as 3/4 also admits 6/8, so even with tag 7 retained,
7394 and 7698 have the same rational readout. The full parser analysis
retains this ambiguity and the zero-denominator exceptions. No transition
rule for "7 waits" has been established.

## The precise gap: closing a round trip need not equalize its two legs

A possible reading of the mirrored path `5|9|5` is two ratio steps:

\[
\frac59\frac95=1,\qquad 5\ne9.
\]

This is a perfectly closed round trip. Its first ratio is still not one.
For every pair of nonzero digits a,b, `(a/b)(b/a)=1`, whether a=b or not.
The product-one fiber is therefore MANY(all 81 admitted ordered pairs).
The 19 words containing zero are excluded from this two-ratio operation.
This [exact test](evidence/ratio-loop-v2.json) retains every case.

The same distinction applies to the quantities being compared for E34:

\[
A=\frac{L''(E,1)}{2},\qquad
B_\sigma=\Omega_{\rm all}\operatorname{Reg}_{\rm full}\sigma,
\qquad D_\sigma=A-B_\sigma.
\]

Here E34 is `y^2=x^3-1156x`. The earlier arithmetic normalization has
Tamagawa product/torsion-square factor `16/16=1`. The value sigma must
be justified as the actual order of Sha, including finiteness. The full
BSD comparison is `D_sigma=0`, equivalently `A/B_sigma=1` when B_sigma
is nonzero. A reciprocal round trip `(A/B_sigma)(B_sigma/A)=1` supplies
no such equality: it already holds for all nonzero unequal A,B_sigma.

The existing A and B1 intervals admit both of these hypothetical pairs:

| A | B1 | A-B1 |
|---|---|---|
| 6.38515 | 6.38515 | 0 |
| 6.38515 | 6.38505 | 0.0001 |

Both can carry the same mirrored-word rule and the same keyring drawing.
They are witnesses to what the interval premises leave undetermined,
not alternate actual L-values or counterexamples to BSD. For the second
pair, `A/B1=127703/127701 != 1`, while its reciprocal round trip is
exactly one.

The missing ingredient is a relation derived from the actual L-function
and arithmetic definitions that rules out that remaining freedom. A
proposed common "seam" would need specified maps to those quantities
and a proved compatibility law forcing A/B_sigma=1. Merely assigning
both measurements to a connected picture does not supply that law.
Another possible sufficient route would prove independently that
A/B1 is an integer: its present narrow interval would then force it
to be one. That integrality is an unresolved proof obligation, not an
assumption supplied by the decimal display. Determining actual Sha
remains a separate obligation.
