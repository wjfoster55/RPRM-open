# Vault, FLICK, three plus one, and relational security: source recovery

Recovered 2026-09-12 for the current YM rail-closure investigation. Historical sources were read only. No old checker, experiment, service, ingestion, or publication was run. The only authored file from this recovery is this report.

## Result and remaining source seam

The recollection has several directly recoverable components: **Vault** as scoped closure plus actual receiver acceptance and one atomic adoption; **FLICK** as one event with multiple temporal faces; **three plus one** as three process layers plus the consequential handoff; and **relational security** as support by compatible relations such that a change requires a coordinated rewrite. The original records keep these concepts connected while distinguishing their contracts.

The exact original phrase **“blockchain without a ledger”**, or a direct original user turn establishing a ledgerless security theorem, was **not recovered in this bounded search**. This is an **OPEN source seam**, not evidence that William never said it or that a relational reconstruction route is false. The supplied recollection remains attributed to the current user. The historical passages below are source evidence, not current instructions.

The recovered software transaction donor explicitly has a conservation ledger and an archive. That establishes what this particular donor implemented. It does not refute a different construction in which compatible current relations determine the current state without a chronological ledger. Such a construction needs its own carrier, compatibility and uniqueness argument, receiver, and soundness boundary; it cannot inherit a security proof merely from the older vocabulary.

## 2. Nothing in the package certifies its surrounding relations by itself

The [Vault Contract](accepted/sources/340d3609c9_VAULT_CONTRACT_1_0.md), lines 107–110, makes the parent seam an additional obligation:

> `parent_seam` is also required as a field. It is `null` only when there is no
> child-to-parent composition in the declared frame. When a closed child is used
> to close a parent, `parent_seam` MUST be a newly executed passing check; child
> Closure alone cannot close the parent.

Lines 148–153 explicitly exclude producer self-tests, hypothetical acceptance, undeclared history, success on repaired/different bytes, isolated component success in place of the composed seam, and receivers that did not execute the continuation.

The older [proof-receipt calculus](accepted/sources/03ba81a68c_RPRM_PROOF_RECEIPT_CALCULUS.md), **S10**, lines 271–275 states:

> SRC is a structural theorem about receipt records. If a checker soundly
> validates each primitive proof token and its sequential rule, then validating
> the flattened composite derives the endpoint conclusion under the retained
> scope. The calculus does not derive checker soundness from the record itself;
> checker semantics are an explicit assumption.

The current [core §16](accepted/sources/2601fdc27e_core.md), **S01**, lines 633–642 preserves the same separation:

> A **claim** is a statement with hypotheses, context, scope and evidence grade.
> A **certificate** is a witness in a specified format with a separately stated
> verification predicate and soundness implication. A **receipt** records the
> subject, method, inputs, result and dependencies of a check. A checker's
> successful output does not prove its soundness merely by existing.
>
> A **seal** binds a claim/context/evidence package to fixed bytes and versions.
> Hash equality is evidence of byte identity under its assumptions. Mathematical
> truth, source authenticity and permission are separate predicates. A local
> content hash alone does not identify an author or grant reuse authority.

**Recovered implication:** a valid local object may be reusable, but validity of its new coupling, complete dependency mask, surrounding compatibility constraints, and receiver continuation still requires an argument. This is a supported synthesis of the cited contracts. It is not an exact recovered user quotation of “nothing self-certifies.”

## 4. Past, present, future: earned distinctions

The [tri-temporal calibration](accepted/sources/a480a92f1c_README.md), **S08**, defines three separate readouts: predecessor/provenance `P`, present observation `C`, and positive-length future distinguishability `F`. Its finite census realizes all eight signatures, including provenance-only `100` and future-only `001`. The existence of one kind of relevance therefore does not force all three.

Exact source passage, lines 51–57:

> The frozen minimum witness has distinguishing language
>
> ```text
> 0*1 = {1, 01, 001, 0001, ...}
> ```
>
> so distinguishing words occur at arbitrarily large lengths. After the first `1`, however, the state pair merges on the next move. No single infinite input has distinguishing prefixes infinitely often.

This is the explicit hostile case against treating arbitrarily long finite continuation evidence as one persistent infinite lineage. The finite report gives three states as the minimum in its signature and 64 labeled witnesses with pair `(0,1)`. Its taxonomy and counterexample do not establish a universal time law.

The [handoff synthesis](accepted/sources/d0eb8eb77e_CHATGPT_HANDOFF.md), **S09**, preserves the interpretation and its condition:

> **6. The `+1` remains relational, not numeric.** “Retention binds past, present, and future” is coherent if retention is a typed lineage/compatibility relation independently read by the receiver. It does not establish a fourth numerical component, a universal hidden unit, or an unavoidable meta-role. The existing duplicate-tag and receiver-authority ablations remain the controls.

A distinct [ternary nested handoff protocol](accepted/sources/33ad47113d_PROTOCOL.md), **S16**, lines 220–225 explicitly marks its rolling `(PAST, PRESENT, FUTURE)` window as **unscored**. A shift requires a supplied `next`; permitted horizon dispositions include periodic wrap, promotion to a coarser clock, generation under a registered rule, and `MISSING_FUTURE`. It supplies no scored evidence that a successor may simply be presumed.

## 5. The recovered security meaning

The original [model v3.3](accepted/sources/a5f6e02f25_RPR_MODEL_V3_3.md), **S05**, line 279 says:

> A retained closure has **relational security**: its current organization is supported by enough compatible relations that leaving requires a coordinated rewrite.

This is the closest recovered passage to the remembered security mechanism. It is about the cost/compatibility of changing a supported organization. It preserves the intended route and should not be dismissed because the same word later named a repository or a receipt format.

The same document's standing rule, line 14, says:

> Everything below is hypothetical unless explicitly labeled as an admitted experimental observation or an established external benchmark.

Its proposed continuation principle at line 307 is explicitly called “a placeholder for an eventual exact functional” at line 309. The status key at line 29 defines `[STRONG]` as originating intuition or coherent working architecture, “not yet derived.” Thus this security passage is an **attributed model hypothesis/interpretation**, not a proved cryptographic bound. No recovered passage establishes adversary resistance, hardness, consensus finality, fault threshold, or authenticity from compatible relations alone.

## 7. Receiver for the YM continuation

The relevant reusable pressure is: **a candidate state must fit the surrounding relations and retain what the next readout needs; independently closed pieces do not automatically close their joint system**. This is supported by the parent-seam requirement, conditional checker soundness, and separate receiver gates.

For any proposed ledgerless current-state reconstruction, the new mathematical receiver must state which joint state is reconstructed, which supplied relations are jointly compatible, why reconstruction is unique up to the admitted equality, and whether the desired observable is constant on every unresolved completion fiber. This is a new typed adapter, not an inherited cryptographic result. Any further YM analytic claim still owes its actual continuum/uniform estimates. This recovery does not adjudicate those new calculations.
