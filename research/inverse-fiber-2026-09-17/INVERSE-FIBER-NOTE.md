## 1. What is being asked

A total finite map `f:X→Z` and a specified output `z`. The inverse is
the complete preimage `{x in X: f(x)=z}`, with missing ports kept joint.

- empty complete fiber → **NONE**
- singleton complete fiber → **ONE(value)**
- complete fiber of size `n>1` → **MANY(family)**
- unfinished scan → **OPEN**

Picking one member of a two-or-more fiber and calling it ONE is the
named hostile. A product of one-port marginals is another hostile. A
stopped search is not MANY of the found witnesses.
