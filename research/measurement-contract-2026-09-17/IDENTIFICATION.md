## Statement

On Bits2:

```text
Count2^{-1}(1) = XOR^{-1}(1) = ((0,1),(1,0)).
```

Disposition of that complete fiber is **MANY**. Decoder
`h(0)=0`, `h(1)=1`, `h(2)=0` satisfies `XOR = h ∘ Count2` on every
point. The Mean2 numeral is not XOR: `(1,1)` has mean `1` and XOR `0`.
The product of the one-bit marginals of the fiber is all four pairs.

That is why Count2/Mean2 identifies XOR and does not identify the pair.
