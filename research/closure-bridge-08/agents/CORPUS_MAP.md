### C1 — Bidirectional completion through one retained middle relation

Take finite typed carriers X,Y,Z and supplied `R⊆X×Y`, `S⊆Y×Z`. Given x,z, retain

`J(x,z)={y∈Y:R(x,y) and S(y,z)}`.

Forward expansion gives admissible y from x; backward expansion gives admissible y into z; their intersection is the middle fiber. If routes carry extra tickets, use `(y,t_R,t_S)` as the middle carrier and enforce their compatibility. This is the smallest exact realization of reasoning both ways together.

Requested readout: existence, unique intermediate value, all witnesses, or a specified Q on them. Rise/fall qualify as exact inverses only when their maps satisfy both inverse equations on the chosen carriers. Otherwise retain the complete preimage. Hostile boss: the left half admits only y=0 and the right half only y=1; both halves exist and the joint route does not. A second boss gives two y routes to one endpoint, separating endpoint completion from trace completion. Basis: O01, witness-aware composition, SOLVE and proof-donut seams. Status: **derived composition of established contracts**, with target adapter OPEN.

### C3 — Complete the local result, then prove its receiving continuation

Separate a local certificate/result carrier from the next-level capability carrier. A pre-carry result is admitted only after its local obligations close. A handoff adapter must then preserve the receiving observation, enabledness and retained successor behavior; promotion is earned only after those equations hold.
