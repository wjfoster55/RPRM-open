Scenarios

Sim

A: fast, but can't climb — leaves the U-tube's far shaft empty, and its surface stays stepped.
B: inertia & splashes, but oscillates and won't flatten.
C: continuous depth, flattens free surfaces, and its pressure term pushes water partway up the far shaft (A/B never do). It still doesn't fully equalize a tall U-tube — see PROBLEMS.md.
D: level-aware. Labels connected water bodies and reads each body's target level straight off (volume ÷ footprint), so a U-tube equalizes to Δh≈0 in tens of steps. Load U-tube and watch both shafts level out.
E: D_fast / E_exact — the cache/skip machinery constrained to reproduce D frame-for-frame (verified by test_frame_equality.js). It re-floods every awake body each relabel frame like D, so it is a faithful mirror, not a speedup.
F: D_fast — the certificate-gated variant that actually skips work (cache connected-body labels + target levels, re-flood only disturbed/uncertified bodies). NOT frame-identical to D: hydrostatically equivalent but with small deviations on fragmenting scenes. Load Multi-body.
