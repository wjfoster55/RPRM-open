# Proposal: discover, reconstruct, lock, transfer

**Not yet an executed or registered study.** This is a template for a prospective third-domain test after the second-domain discovery work.

## Claim under test

A portable Process Mechanics procedure can identify/use task-relevant distinctions and coordinate observations in a new physical domain without selecting its rules after seeing the new task's scored outcomes.

This is stronger than repeating code on new random seeds in the same simulator, but weaker than proving a universal physical theory. Decide whether the claim concerns workflow transfer, an unchanged observation-selection algorithm, or an exact computation kernel. Do not switch among them after seeing the results.

## A / B / C

A: existing pressurized-network inference studies, retained as discovery and existing validation within that setup.

B: conservative tracer transport with slow exchange, used for independent reconstruction from domain equations and conventional controls. Compare with the A template only after describing the B problem in its own terms. Any feature or rule selected using B outcomes is discovery-developed.

C: a preselected shallow-water propagation/detection task, with scored episodes held out until the common method and adapter are frozen. A small one-dimensional shallow-water benchmark is the numerical entry point; do not begin with full flood maps. C's cases must not be chosen because they resemble a favorable A or B result.

The letters identify stages of validation, not RPRM process digits. Parallel B exploration is compatible with ongoing A work; C can have a prewritten plan and separate evaluator, but its outcomes should not leak into method selection.

## Freeze the core

Freeze the receiver contract, permitted representation/refinement procedure, selection logic, cache-invalidation semantics, stopping/reopening rule, error/coverage treatment, computational budget rules, primary endpoints, baseline slate, missing-data behavior, exclusions, and rule for unsuccessful cases. Hash/pin the implementation and protocol.

For a strictly unchanged-algorithm claim, the selector's executable logic and hyperparameter-selection rules remain fixed. For a broader workflow-transfer claim, list exactly which decisions the workflow is allowed to derive from domain inputs and how. An unconstrained instruction to find something useful is not a frozen algorithm.

## Permit a declared adapter

The adapter may change physical equations/solver, units, geometry, available sensors, observation functional, time scales, known initial/boundary conditions and labels appropriate to C. Calibration may use a separate predeclared calibration set and fixed selection procedure. Audit that the target family is representable, or identify deliberate out-of-model stress separately.

No access to held-out target states, future signals, scoring labels or outcome summaries during adaptation. Do not retune a tolerance, gap, candidate family, margin threshold, choice of sensor or fallback on the scored bank.

## What outcome-blind does and does not mean

It does not mean ignorance of C's physics. Documentation, published verification examples, analytical solutions and instrument/solver limits must be understood. Adapter smoke tests can use separate known cases.

It means the scientific decisions are fixed before the scored outcomes are accessible. Use separate evaluator files/processes with sanitized policy inputs and a restricted interface. File names and metadata must not reveal the answer. A chat instruction not to peek is not the same as enforced separation.

Commit predictions/selection traces before the evaluator reports results. Hashes record a version; they do not alone establish that nobody inspected outcomes previously. Document access and provenance.

## Comparators and controls

Use ordinary same-information baselines, equal-budget fixed schedules or fixed query order as appropriate, and a conventional domain-aware method. Include an ablation that removes the hypothesized enabling relation without quietly changing data access. An oracle using the full hidden state is an explanatory upper reference, not a fair deployable comparator.

Use at least one structurally different source of numerical/model error across domains; running the same synthetic generator with renamed variables is not independent cross-domain corroboration. In C, distinguish simulator integration errors from observation policy errors by refinement/analytic reference tests.

## Outcomes

Score the declared target, not a proxy that became convenient. Preserve no-answer, multiple-answer, false unique answer, solver failure, late answer and uncertainty width. Report per-domain and per-regime paired differences. Do not pool different tasks into a synthetic universal success percentage.

Select the statistical unit as an independently generated physical preparation/trajectory family, not every frame, sampling clock, candidate, or repeat of the same episode. Predeclare uncertainty estimation appropriate to the unit.

For a look-away endpoint, commit predictions or justified bounds over a future interval from prefix-only evidence, then evaluate withheld readings. A hidden recorder evaluates prediction without removing the physical measuring interaction; a claim about sensor back-action requires a different experiment.

## After results

A successful transfer supports the declared scope. A failure identifies where the shared template, adapter, data coverage or solver assumptions need work; retain the first score. Repairs create a new version and require a new untouched confirmation set. Exploratory failure analysis remains valuable but cannot replace the first outcome.
