## 1. The exact spectral product rule

Let `u_J,v_K` be kinetic eigenblocks with eigenvalues `lambda_J,lambda_K`.
The Laplacian product rule gives

```text
T(u_J v_K)=lambda_J u_J v_K+lambda_K u_J v_K-Gamma(u_J,v_K).
```

For any complete output Peter--Weyl block `L`, therefore,

```text
P_L Gamma(u_J,v_K)
 = (lambda_J+lambda_K-lambda_L) P_L(u_J v_K),

P_L B(u_J,v_K)
 = [(lambda_J+lambda_K-lambda_L)/lambda_L]P_L(u_J v_K)
                       for lambda_L>0.                 (SP1)
```

The scalar block is removed by `Q`, rather than divided by zero. This
is an exact identity of functions/coefficient matrices. It retains the
direction and sign of every channel. A positive sign occurs below the
input energy sum, a negative sign above it, and zero at equality.

Several full Fourier blocks can share the same numerical eigenvalue.
Their support and coefficient matrices are still different occurrences.
An energy-only spectral label is enough to specify the multiplier in
SP1; it is insufficient for the anchored norm and conditional-support
receiver unless those additional labels are retained as well.

## 7. What counts as a valid sign gain

For a fixed input pair, SP1 splits the product into output blocks and
multiplies each block by its exact scalar. The DC2 energy weight then
cancels the inverse energy:

```text
sum_(L:e in supp L) lambda_L ||P_L B(u_J,v_K)||_A
 =sum_(L:e in supp L)
      |lambda_J+lambda_K-lambda_L| ||P_L(u_Jv_K)||_A.     (SP15)
```

Thus the denominator in SP1 cannot be counted again as a free small
factor after applying this norm. A positive resolvent shift retains the
factor `lambda_L/(lambda_L+z)`, whose uniform limitation is SP14.

For several input pairs, a valid cancellation must occur after their
coefficients have been placed in the **same complete output block**:

```text
coefficient_L(Gamma(u,v))
 =sum_(J,K) (lambda_J+lambda_K-lambda_L)
                     coefficient_L(u_J v_K).           (SP16)
```

One must prove a bound on this sum of matrices before taking its trace
norm. Matching only the numerical energy `lambda_L` does not combine
different supports or different full Fourier blocks. Opposite signs on
the `9` and `13` channels in SP2 furnish no such cancellation in DC2.
The pointwise gain SP10 and the Fourier sum SP11 are both correct
because they answer different questions.

The useful missing retained quantity is therefore the output channel
with its coefficient and support, not merely a signed aggregate or an
operation's answer at one configuration/scale. The exact local formula
can inform the source constant; a new generic nonlinear bound still
requires actual same-block coefficient control or another proved norm.
