## Established primary literature

- [Kogut and Susskind, Hamiltonian formulation of Wilson's lattice gauge theories (1975)](https://journals.aps.org/prd/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevD.11.395):
  established lattice Hamiltonian framework, already part of the accepted
  source. This continuation does not invent that framework.
- [Kato, Perturbation Theory for Linear Operators, Chapter VII](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-642-66282-9):
  established analytic perturbation and Riesz-projection framework. The
  quantitative contour, nonzero-mean argument and complete L2 tail bound
  used here are written explicitly in CONNECTED_VACUUM.md. The author also
  opened an [academic-hosted book copy](https://webhomes.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/papers/kato1.pdf).
  The independent reviewer verified the direct argument, not the entire book.
- [Michael Taylor, Short Course on Pseudodifferential Operators, I.3, I.4 and I.8](https://mtaylor.web.unc.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/16915/2022/05/psidolect.pdf):
  established elliptic regularity and compact-manifold Sobolev mapping facts
  used for the fixed-graph smooth analytic bootstrap. This does not supply
  graph-uniform constants.
- [Mathur and Sreeraj, Canonical transformations and loop formulation of SU(N) lattice gauge theories (2015)](https://journals.aps.org/prd/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevD.92.125018):
  its abstract explicitly distinguishes magnetic terms proportional to
  1/g^2 and electric terms proportional to g^2. In the usual fixed-lattice
  convention this makes their relative strength proportional to g^-4,
  up to the normalization factors. Small r here means a weak magnetic
  perturbation of the electric Hamiltonian; it must not be mislabeled a
  proof in the continuum weak-gauge-coupling limit. We do not supply the
  full matching of lattice spacing, couplings and physical units.
- [Jaffe and Witten, Quantum Yang–Mills Theory](https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/yangmills.pdf):
  established problem statement requiring a nontrivial continuum quantum
  theory on R^4 with a positive mass gap. Fixed compact graph spectra and
  the present vacuum witness do not fulfill that requirement.
