## 1. Obtain complete coverage before trusting a finite certificate

The earlier arithmetic proof explicitly preserves the obstruction:
`3Z` surjects onto every `Z/2^nZ` while remaining a proper subgroup of `Z`
[B1, line 108]. Thus all binary levels can answer their admitted quotient
questions correctly while missing the integral-generator question.

The current generator continuation supplies a different argument [B3,
lines 54–157]. Its written all-input duplication inequality bounds the
canonical-height error. If `P=kQ+T` with odd `|k|>1`, then `|k|>=3` and
height quadraticity forces `H(Q)<=20`. Only after this reduction does it
enumerate the complete 511 reduced rational abscissas. Every surviving
nontorsion point has free class `+P` or `-P`, excluding every proper odd
index at once. The cutoff is a consequence of the universal inequality;
it is not a completeness assumption attached to a search.

**YM use:** finitely many connected coefficients, spin labels, or graph sizes
can be exact without covering the connected remainder. A useful finite
certificate needs an all-order majorant that covers its omitted terms, with
its parameter and graph quantifiers written explicitly. For a graph-family
conclusion, a bound deteriorating with graph size leaves that conclusion open.
The existing obstruction `(1+d^4)^N-1` [Y1, Section 1] already distinguishes
the fixed two-square bound from a repeatable family estimate. This is a
method transfer, not an asserted equivalence between odd index and graph size.

## 3. Keep signed coefficients and certify the entire conditional remainder

The derivative certificate [B2, Sections “All-term coverage” and “Signed
combination”] derives `|a_n|<=2n` for every index, bounds the entire suffix,
and combines a signed finite head with that radius. Negative coefficients
reverse interval endpoints. Its ledger separates coefficient tail,

Deleting the all-term premise leaves the finite head intact but makes the
infinite-sum promotion **OPEN**. The later factor proof [B4] likewise uses
an all-iteration height tail and AGM brackets, rather than small observed
successive differences.

A precise YM adaptation is available without identifying the vacuum with
`exp(-V)`. Suppose a new proof supplies

```text
F=log rho=F_head+R,
b_(B,j) >= sup_(omega,omega' differing only at j)
             osc_(U_B)[F_head(U_B,omega')-F_head(U_B,omega)],
epsilon_(B,j) >= the same oscillation for R.
```

By subadditivity of oscillation and the accepted tilt lemma [Y1,
Sections 2–3], the actual conditional total variation obeys

```text

```

This is a written consequence of the displayed premises. It does not supply
those missing vacuum bounds. If instead the expansion is for `log psi_0`,
both oscillation bounds acquire factor two. Terms entirely outside the patch
cancel exactly before estimation. A global supremum bound on `R` may give a
valid but volume-dependent answer; the useful family receiver requires
adequate sums over changed exterior occurrences, not only a separate finite
bound for each occurrence. Inferring a spectral estimate still requires the
chosen influence theorem's actual hypotheses and update-rate normalization.
