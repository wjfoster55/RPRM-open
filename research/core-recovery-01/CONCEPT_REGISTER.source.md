**Proposed bridge evidence:** `FORMAL_BRIDGE_NOTE.md` §§2–6 and CB02–CB10/CB12.

**Do not conflate:** displayed value; cycle/winding; construction depth; learned
access; uncertainty; elapsed time; computation cost; or the authority-inspection
sense of “prestige” in Absolute Distinction.
