## Exact current research seam

Parity supplies a compact family closed under conjunction and existential projection. The new proof and pilot test that family. A general Boolean relation need not remain affine: replacing OR3 by its affine hull admits `000`, and replacing exactly-one by its affine hull admits `111`. Those errors can turn an incompatible join into a false SAT answer.

The next useful target is an **exact mixed affine/non-affine boundary representation** with recognition, projection, conjunction and reference transport charged to the algorithm. Preserve a clause or decision structure when affine rows are insufficient. Compare against a conventional solver with the same parity capability. A polynomial bound must cover intermediate representation size, operation count and bit cost as input grows; small final output and finite fixture success do not supply it.

This is a concrete continuation point. It does not depend on the historical idea having only one interpretation, and it does not discard the exact affine results when the broader problem remains open.
