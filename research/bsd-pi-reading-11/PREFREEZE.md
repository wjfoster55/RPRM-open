User proposal: the displayed E34 distance bounds near2.0340 may be 'pi
zipped up', with decimal shifts, zeros, side/middle digits and carry.
This is an abductive proposal. We have no user-specified executable rule
covering all described digit operations. Keep that larger grammar OPEN.

Freeze these separate candidates and receivers:

1. Formatting/carry: both original endpoint words have three zeros; every
   number in their interval rounds to2.0340 at four decimal places.
   Compare fresh depth8 and depth9 height enclosures and display widths.
   Endpoint strings are two certified bounds on one real number.
2. Endpoint-midpoint chart: interpret34 as(A,m)=(3,4). Solve B=2m−A=5.
   Check the complete seven-record old zip carrier, all inverse charts,
   and the different interpretation34=(A,B), which is outside that carrier.
3. Literal decimal shifting: d=10^k*pi or d=10^k*(4−pi). Test k=−6..6
   by rigorous rational intervals and state an order argument for other k.
   These are assistant-selected simple interpretations, not the user's
   complete proposed RPRM operation.
4. Optional unit-carry/square reading: d²−1=pi, motivated by the squared
   height readout and a unit carry. This too is an explicit assistant
   candidate, not attributed as the user's intended equation.
5. Known digit maps: compare P,N,T,M, identity, reversals and cyclic
   rotations of the two original fixed-width words with the same-width
   certified initial pi window. Record the finite family even if no match.
   No arbitrary fitted constants or remote digit-search are admitted.
6. Source-rooted pi geometry: independently derive the actual period/pi
   identity with its exact scale. Do not transfer it to the distance without
   a further relation. The invariant BSD regulator is distinct from the
   distance of a selected ordered basis.
