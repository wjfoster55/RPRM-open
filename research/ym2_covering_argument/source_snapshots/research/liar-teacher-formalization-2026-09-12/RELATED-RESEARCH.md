## What transfers usefully

**1. A compact answer can remain valid while a stronger answer needs reopening.**
The reviewed E5 construction retains
`R = mP + T + 2^n R_n`. The quotient label `(m,T)` supports its quotient
readout; the remainder `R_n` supports reconstruction and refinement. The
identity and `2P` collide modulo doubles but separate modulo quadruples.
Removing the remainder does not falsify the coarse quotient; it removes the
information that selects the finer answer. For a teacher protocol, distinguish
the current question's sufficient record from the extra record needed by the
next question. A same-answer comparison is insufficient to justify reuse after
changing the question or action. Source:
[E5 readback and controls](/C:/github/RPRM-open/research/bsd-e5-completion/supplied_review/BSD_E5_REVIEW/input/BSD_E5_CODEX_TEST_01_RETURN/RETURN_TO_WILLIAM.md:113).

**2. Transport the operation and readout with the reference.**
E5's five-bit encoding checks every one of the 1,024 operation pairs; equal
cardinality alone would not show an operation-preserving representation.
Its coherent basis/readout transport passes while a stale readout fails.
The YM scalar note supplies exact moving-reference formulas:
`J_a(x)=a-x`, `J_b(J_a(x))=x+(b-a)`, and
`d_(k+1)-d_k=(x_(k+1)-x_k)-(a_(k+1)-a_k)` for `d_k=x_k-a_k`.
The reference's own motion is retained data. Under `F(x)=x^2`, the difference
is `F(a+d)-F(a)=2ad+d^2`; a record containing only `d` loses the dependence on
`a`. These give useful controls for the moving-reference proposal without
identifying reflection, recharting, and reversing time. Sources:
[E5 transport](/C:/github/RPRM-open/research/bsd-e5-completion/supplied_review/BSD_E5_REVIEW/input/BSD_E5_CODEX_TEST_01_RETURN/RETURN_TO_WILLIAM.md:113),
[retained references](/C:/github/RPRM-open/research/ym2_signed_differences/SIGNED_SEMANTICS.md:96),
[moving-reference contract](/C:/github/RPRM-open/research/ym2_signed_differences/SIGNED_SEMANTICS.md:194).

**3. Local compatibility needs one compatible global witness.**
The E5 result combines necessary local survival with actual rational
representatives for all eight surviving classes, plus an exclusion/coverage
argument. The cube represents classes already justified arithmetically; its
shape supplies no extra existence proof. In the two-cell YM model, separate
loop readings omit their relative alignment on the shared edge. Two states
have the same present energy and instantaneous transfer but different second
derivatives of magnetic energy. Joining the cells therefore requires the same
shared witness and a common transported frame. This suggests keeping teacher
observations as constraints on one source, with source/channel occurrence
identities, rather than collecting independently satisfiable answers. Sources:
[E5 local/global closure](/C:/github/RPRM-open/research/bsd-e5-completion/supplied_review/BSD_E5_REVIEW/input/BSD_E5_CODEX_TEST_01_RETURN/RETURN_TO_WILLIAM.md:36),
[YM shared-edge future witness](/C:/github/RPRM-open/research/ym2_spatial_joint/RETURN_TO_WILLIAM.md:15).

**4. A future receiver can expose a missing relation absent from present views.**
The YM two-cell repair retains a joint configuration and conjugate momenta
with a closed Hamiltonian on its regular domain. A later ordered third loop
needs orientation: pairwise angles can agree for mirror arrangements while
an ordered triple readout differs. The outer product `W=UV` likewise does not
determine fine inside energy; retaining `U` permits the exact reopening
`V=U^-1 W`. The transferable design is to seek a short admitted continuation
that distinguishes a proposed merged pair, then retain the specific missing
relation or return its complete compatible readout family. Source:
[YM stronger receivers and reopening](/C:/github/RPRM-open/research/ym2_spatial_joint/RETURN_TO_WILLIAM.md:104).

**5. Count independence at the premise and implementation levels.**
The E5 reviewer used a separately written numerical method with a different
quadrature/error construction and imported no submitted implementation. Both
methods still use the same analytic identity and all-term coefficient theorem.
Their agreement checks that evaluation; it is not two independent proofs of
those shared premises. The YM stacking example similarly separates independent
copies from repeated copies of one bit: equal marginals have different sum
variances because the coupling differs. For liar/teacher checks, repeated
reports or views of one source do not automatically create independent truth
evidence. Sources:
[E5 independent-check boundary](/C:/github/RPRM-open/research/bsd-e5-completion/supplied_review/BSD_E5_REVIEW/REVIEW.md:49),
[YM joint stacking controls](/C:/github/RPRM-open/research/ym2_signed_differences/SIGNED_SEMANTICS.md:156).

**6. Preserve the supported part when a coverage argument fails.**
E5's missing-tail control leaves its finite-head interval available and the
infinite-sum claim OPEN. YM proves that six edge-disjoint loops make one

bound, not the actual physical gap. Its conditional cancellation result has an
explicit supplied factorization premise, still unproved for the actual vacuum.
A failed bound or exhausted certificate search should therefore identify its
missing obligation and retain already-supported readouts. Sources:
[E5 control dispositions](/C:/github/RPRM-open/research/bsd-e5-completion/supplied_review/BSD_E5_REVIEW/REVIEW.md:96),

## Limits and next use in the parent task

These sources support fixtures for coherent/stale frame transport,
same-present/different-future states, repeated-source correlation, complete
joint witnesses, and scoped reopening. They do not establish that an arbitrary
3+1 or 7+1 arrangement is an error-correcting code or a truth guarantee; the
parent must supply its parity checks, distance/error model, channels, and
receiver. They also supply no P versus NP conclusion. Construction, checking,
stored data, access, refinement, and output costs must all be counted before a
complexity claim can follow from a compact reusable object.
