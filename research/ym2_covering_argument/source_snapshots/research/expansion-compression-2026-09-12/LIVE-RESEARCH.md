## 2. Yang–Mills: subtract reducible combinations, retain connected ones

The completed signed-difference packet writes each auxiliary heat kernel as
`q_s=1+r_s` with zero-integral residual. Gauge integration kills every correction
support with an open end. Only four nonempty support shapes survive on the
two-cell graph. But an absolute envelope on larger graphs obeys

```text
eta_G(d) ≥ (1+d^4)^N−1
```

when N edge-disjoint square cycles are present. At the packet's `d=3/5`, six
cycles already make this envelope exceed one. This defeats that particular
positive lower-bound inference, not the true gap. It identifies the cost of
counting all disconnected combinations by absolute magnitude.
[Support-growth proof](../ym2_signed_differences/NEXT_CONNECTED_OBLIGATION.md),
lines 8–43.

The **new active packet has a concrete algebraic compression**. Its actual
two-cell ground state has coefficients

```text

u1=(a+b)/6,
u2=(a²+b²−1/2)/96+ab/39+w/351,
||R3||₂ ≤ |r|³/[2√2(1−|r|)]  for |r|<1,
```

where `a=Sc(U)`, `b=Sc(V)`, `w=Sc(UV)` and
`r=beta/(alpha hbar²)`. Passing to the log density replaces the second-order
coefficient by `2u2−u1²`: the reducible product contribution is subtracted.
For edge-disjoint plaquettes the pair term cancels exactly; adjacent pairs
retain **`(4w−3ab)/702`**. This is an exact connected-coefficient calculation
through order two, analogous to the purpose of cumulants. It is not an
all-order cluster-expansion theorem. Later orders can connect distant cells
through intermediate plaquettes.
[Connected-vacuum derivation](../ym2_connected_vacuum/CONNECTED_VACUUM.md),
lines 9–29, 145–210, and 245–318.


**actual vacuum retains joint orientation that separate loop traces lose**.

bound. The new mathematical reviews accept this written argument at its

[Witness and complete configuration fiber](../ym2_connected_vacuum/VACUUM_SEPARATING_WITNESS.md),

These are useful shared questions, with distinct domain proofs behind them:

1. **Specify the next receiver before compressing.** Present readout agreement

2. **Keep shared occurrences joint.** Repeated-source correlations, a shared
   SU(2) edge, and temporary water support cannot be replaced by independent
   marginal summaries. Supply the coupling or boundary relation explicitly.
3. **Retain the reopening information.** A quotient remainder, an orientation
   invariant, or full dynamic state can carry what a smaller present summary
   forgets. Return a complete compatible family when a unique reopen is not
   supported.
4. **Compress combinations only after proving their cancellation or
   irrelevance.** The log-density calculation removes disconnected pair
   products; conditional normalization cancels exterior-only factors. Neither
   operation licenses deleting connected interactions or unbounded remainders.
5. **Make finite-to-family coverage explicit.** E5's proved height cutoff closes
   one unbounded index question. YM's shrinking radius exposes an unresolved
   uniformity question. A panel of successful fluid scenes does not cover all
   admitted update geometries.
6. **Measure the entire computation.** State count, summary size, variance,
   spectral gap, update-clock normalization, preprocessing, retained backing,
   refinement, and elapsed time answer different questions. For example, the
   exact product-bit control has `2^n` states but a per-coordinate-rate
   resampling gap of one for every finite n; its worsening global comparison
   bound does not make the actual gap worsen.
   [Stacking control](../ym2_signed_differences/STACKING_AND_SCALING.md).
