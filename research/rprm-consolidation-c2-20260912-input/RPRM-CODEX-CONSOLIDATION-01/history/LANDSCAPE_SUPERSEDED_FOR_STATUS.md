The dynamic counterexample is the cleanest result in the new return. In the posted model-B fixture, the same container and volume 180 give the same summary but different H=300 breach outcomes: tall arrangement breaches, flat arrangement does not. The 84/144 disagreement count is 58.3% of deliberately constructed equal-summary pairs, not a prevalence estimate for arbitrary fluids.

The witness varies discarded spatial arrangement. Its Python rollout harness supplies no differing initial velocity arrays; the JS bridge normalizes velocities before optional overrides. Therefore the demonstrated witness does not justify calling velocity alone the repair. Keeping the complete relevant dynamical state is a sufficient fallback, but neither it nor the velocity field has been proved minimal. Appending the final answer itself distinguishes the question but says nothing about cheap discovery of that answer.

The broadphase result is also useful, but its metric is **certification rate**: seed7 gives 51,742 versus 46,577 certified island-steps out of 70,221, or 73.6845% versus 66.3292%. The relative ratio is 1.11089; the absolute difference is 7.35535 percentage points. These are not runtime ratios. The runner first evolves a full reference trajectory and then computes potential skipped-contact/body fractions from the records; these are not measured end-to-end savings from a deployed skipping engine.

The comparator is an implemented CA-plus-sleeping rule in a local disk simulator, not a direct Box2D/Bullet/TGS benchmark. The saved output has 5,754 P-only successes and 589 comparator-only successes: P does not dominate pointwise. The 0.1% error-budget sensitivity is for **CA+sleeping**, not the unguarded naive velocity-sleep baseline. Its FP denominator is all island-step decisions. Baseline thresholds are selected from this exposed suite; this is not an untouched prospective validation.

Zero observed false certifications across the reported seeds is useful evidence. It is not an all-input guarantee. That stronger claim needs proved impulse, acceleration, timestep and numerical assumptions for the actual discrete update. An unchanged contact-pair set also does not alone justify freezing normals, separation, impulses, or full dynamics.

Witnessed invalidation removes 490 timer-driven settled reverifications in the reported suite; it preserves the dominant flood work and shows no meaningful speed win. Preserve that result. Naming a touched cell is a useful explanation; a claim of a minimal or complete causal certificate needs a separate statement about the supported mutations.

A defensible provisional thesis is: “Task-relative retained representations support exact reuse under stated operation contracts. Constructed examples expose both useful reuse and insufficiency. In a local collision-transfer study, a relative-motion guard increases observed certification rate over a specified comparator, with no observed false certifications on the reported suites; runtime advantage and universal soundness are separate obligations.”

Do not present a measured inverse law between incumbent sophistication and payoff, or a numerical percentage of novelty. Two different comparisons and an informal prior-art judgment do not estimate either.

## 4. SAT: the next computational question is already defined

SAT v1 has useful recognition examples and a real limit: its pair/triple grouping misses the protected four-clause XOR. Permitting four factors recovers the parity relation. Eliminating every variable to TRUE in an isolated satisfiable block is a different achievement.

The saved review also shows wider grouping is not uniformly better under a budget: in stress, B decided 21/27 and C 13/27, with eight decisions becoming resource limits. The measuring repairs and four matched variants are already in Task02: W3, W4, W3-T (bounded temporary exact tables), and W4-I (incidence-indexed selection).

The three-color star is the most concrete connection to the recovered cube: eliminating an internal X from X≠A, X≠B, X≠C leaves a genuine joint boundary relation, “A,B,C use at most two colors.” Every pair projection allows every pair; their conjunction loses the obstruction. Test compact relations when they overlap, not just in isolation.

The next success condition is not P=NP. It is a checked improvement, or a clear limit, in discovering and carrying exact boundary relations with total costs and witness reconstruction included. General polynomial-time claims additionally require uniform size and time bounds on compilation, intermediate representations, nonemptiness and recovery. C1 is a correctness ingredient, not such a complexity theorem.

## 6. Yang–Mills: make the missing bridge physical

The retrieved photon example correctly includes relative direction in composite invariant mass; the cube detects the omitted three-way coefficient. That is a useful calibration of relational information, not yet a Yang–Mills field model.

A simple boundary illustration: for two opposite photons of equal energy ε in c=1 units, the same kinematic rule gives total invariant mass 2ε. It is positive for each ε>0 but can approach zero. This is why “relations can yield positive composite mass” does not by itself establish a mass gap. This observation is not a refutation of the proposed broader RPRM research direction.

A next physical bridge should name the gauge theory/model, actual state and dynamics, gauge-invariant observable, retained map, and energy scale. A finite discretization can be a development tool. Any eventual mass-gap argument must distinguish physical excitation energy from finite-volume/cutoff artifacts and prove the appropriate continuum/infinite-volume statement. A classical trajectory, arbitrary graph spectrum, or energy assigned to a ‘seam’ does not supply that automatically.

For the first bounded task, choose and justify one observable-level experiment or lemma. Define what a failure would teach about the retained representation before building a large simulator.
