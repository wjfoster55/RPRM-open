# Visual review

Root inspected the final 2800 by 1200 PNG at its final layout. The three
panels share a length scale. The initial and refined regions are both two
metres wide, while the extended region is four metres wide. Cell widths
are one, one and one half metre, respectively. These are illustrative
lengths, not numerical lattice parameters used in the research proof.

The refined caption was split over two lines to stay inside the canvas.
All captions, dimension arrows and explanatory text are visible. The
picture separates increasing spatial extent from decreasing cell size;
it does not claim that redrawing a grid transports the operator or its
couplings. The editable SVG and drawing source are included. Regeneration
uses already available Matplotlib; mathematical replay uses only Python's
standard library.
