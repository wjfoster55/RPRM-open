## Anti-Pi status restored

Nothing in the sealed work refutes anti-Pi as a research direction. It splits
the phrase into four non-equivalent objects:

1. `P(pi-window)` — exact dependent digit involution.
2. `N(pi-window)` — exact dependent modular-negation involution.
3. `4 - pi` — exact dependent arithmetic complement.
4. An independently specified source/carrier deserving the name anti-Pi —
   **open; no such source has yet been frozen or observed.**

The first three are useful shadows. Their dependence is not a dismissal; it is
the property that tells us what kind of information they can and cannot add.

## New finite probe licensed by this audit

`1234679` deserves a bounded test because its relationship to the P-pair
partition is unusually clean: applying P exchanges membership of the single
orbit `{2,8}` and preserves membership across the other four P-orbits. It is
also fixed as a set by `T=PN`, so its P- and N-image sets coincide. The digit
support of the displayed `4-pi` prefix has the same two properties while
occupying the **opposite** representative, 8 rather than 2, of that half-filled
orbit. This supports a precise candidate name—**T-fixed opposite-half
pair**—without calling it anti-Pi yet.

The previously tied `WBR-T05-DYADIC-ANCESTRY-REFLECTION` boss is also still
live. It was not selected by the old canonical tie-break; it was not killed.
It tests the finite `1 -> 3 -> 7 -> 15` ancestry and interval reflection on the
frozen `D4` grid, which directly addresses the paired-tree/seam intuition in a
carrier where inverse fibers can be checked.
