Three things to try

Hear it if you like.
See the relation either way.

  1. Move up an octave. The piano roll moves and the sound gets higher. The pitch-class dial stays the same.
  2. Rotate the rhythm. Notes wrap across the edge of the bar. Their cyclic spacing stays fixed.
  3. Unlink one note. Change its pitch. The interval pattern changes, while the other five notes stay put.
About the source, the lenses, and the sound

Six labelled notes keep their pitches, identities, and onsets. The piano roll shows pitch and time, the chromatic dial reduces pitches modulo twelve, and the rhythm wheel reduces onsets modulo sixteen. Those views each keep different information; the source retains the full phrase.

Optional sound uses a soft synthesized tone in twelve-tone equal temperament, with A4 at 440 Hz. Play starts a repeating four-beat bar. Edits restart the bar so scheduled notes match the new phrase; leaving the page stops playback.

The gap multiset is a summary, not a complete rhythm. These are deliberately specified music and display maps. No claim about preference or perception follows from the shared shapes.
