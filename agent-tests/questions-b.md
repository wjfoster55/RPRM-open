# Handbook-only transfer assessment B

Read only the supplied AGENT_HANDBOOK.md. No companion file, corpus, other task
or web source is available for this assessment. Use that document and ordinary
mathematical reasoning. Answer all12 questions and identify any handbook gap.

1. Real ports L,R,u,x satisfy x=(1-u)L+uR. Given L=3,u=0,x=3, solve for R
   completely; repeat with x=4. Explain why dividing by u is inappropriate.
2. The source fiber has two witnesses {a,b}; beta maps both to one abstract
   witness z and the abstract fiber is {z}. The abstract fiber equals the
   image of the source fiber. Which existence, uniqueness and reconstruction
   conclusions transfer? What extra property would change the uniqueness result?
3. Q takes values true and integer1 in two states. A typed receiver regards
   them as distinct, but the implementation compares them as equal numerals.
   What contract must be repaired, and what would a faithful quotient require?
4. A finite search's admitted hypothesis set is empty after the observations.
   May the system claim a particular readout is true because all surviving
   hypotheses agree on it? State the correct disposition and next obligation.
5. C merges x,y with O both0. Action a sends x to r and y to s; action b sends
   r to t and s to s. O(r)=O(s)=0, O(t)=1. All other actions self-loop and are
   total. The retained initial summary distinguishes r,s,t from x,y. Give a
   shortest distinguishing word for x,y and explain why present-only and
   one-question repair differ from full stable refinement.
6. Two nondeterministic sources have successor lists [A,A,B] and [A,B]. Under
   set-valued semantics may they merge on this step (assuming equal current
   observations)? What changes under multiplicity or probability semantics?
7. A four-report model guarantees at most one wrong binary report, but the
   observed reports are 0,0,1,1. What conclusion is justified? Do not silently
   relax the premise or select a majority that does not exist.
8. An endpoint compiler maps both a one-step identity program and a two-step
   identity program to the same function. Is it incorrect? Give two receivers
   with different answers and identify what the second compiler must retain.
9. A large-number constructor lets you compute a parity bit instantly from
   its tag. Creating that tag cost the entire original expensive computation.
   State the information claim and the missing performance claim. Design a
   fair cost comparison in two sentences.
10. Provide a complete parameterization of all integer pairs x,y with x-y=7,
    and prove both directions of its coverage. Is this an enumeration, a
    certificate, or a proof of an arbitrary integer-solving procedure?
11. Two independently coded checkers agree on every supplied row of a
    synthetic psychology table. State exactly what kind of result this is,
    and propose one meaningful external validation test plus a hostile control.
    May the table be used to diagnose a person's beliefs from its internal labels?
12. Summarize the unifying purpose of RPRM for a skeptical reader in five
    sentences. Include a valid mathematical bridge, a concrete new question
    the framework helps expose, its relation to existing mathematics and the
    status of physical interpretations. Do not claim universality or novelty
    merely from shared terminology.
