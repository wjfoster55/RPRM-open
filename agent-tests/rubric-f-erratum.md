# Assessment F rubric correction

The frozen rubric's question 6 incorrectly gives the derived bound as 32.
For the supplied `n=2,s=2,d=2`, the handbook's formula gives
`M=2*n*(s+d)=2*2*(2+2)=16`.

The respondent correctly computed 16, with `D(16)=180`, and independently
derived the complete root fiber. This error was identified during grading
after the response. The frozen rubric is retained unchanged; the correct
answer receives credit. The criterion for question 6 is interpreted with
M=16. No question, answer, acceptance threshold or other criterion changed.

This is an error in the assessment key, not in the handbook's formula or
the executable decision. The result record identifies the correction and
retains both the frozen rubric and this erratum.
