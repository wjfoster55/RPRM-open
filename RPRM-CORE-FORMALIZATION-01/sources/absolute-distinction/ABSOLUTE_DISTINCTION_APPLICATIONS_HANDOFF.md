## Meaning to preserve

The retrieved sources define Absolute Distinction as an inspection policy:
no relevant step is exempt because it is familiar, prestigious, aesthetically
appealing, or RPRM-branded. It does not guarantee an unbiased observer or make
all existing distinctions wrong. Human judgment still selects the question,
source, proposed representations, and experiments.

A suggested plain-language formulation, for the owning thread to consider:

> Make the distinctions that frame a problem available for examination, rather
> than letting inherited categories decide in advance which relationships can
> matter. Keep the meanings and conditions explicit while testing alternatives.

This is a paraphrase, not a replacement definition or a proven new result.

## Suggested division between the papers

An editorial distinction, not a formal claim that their subjects are disjoint:

- **Process Mechanics:** what a representation must preserve when used to
  answer, continue, compose, or justify reuse.
- **Absolute Distinction:** how the starting distinctions, roles, and allowed
  transformations themselves are exposed to inspection, then revised or retained.

Reusing Process Mechanics' proofs can avoid repeatedly justifying the same
abstract preservation rule. It does not eliminate the obligation to establish
that a new domain instantiates the rule's hypotheses. The new paper should say
what operational guidance it adds beyond the published paper's existing
human-guided proposal stage.

## Three concrete exports from the applications lane

### 1. Coordinate choice versus a physical distinction

FL1.1's periodic grid example represents columns `{62,63,0,1}` on a 64-column
ring using one unwrapped interval `[62,66)` modulo 64. The original description
expanded to the full axis. On its stated seam case, actual cell calculations
fell from 21,504 to 9,088 with identical full-state evolution. Translating the
scene does not change the corresponding aligned dynamics.

**What this supplies:** an exposed, finite numerical example where improving a
representation removes unnecessary work without adding physical dimensions.
**What it does not supply:** universal speedup, proof that dual charts are never
useful, reduced-memory fluid dynamics, or an unseen Absolute Distinction test.
Source: `FLUID_LAB_RETURN_FL1_1.md`, sections Pre-change and B; subsequent hub
replay is already recorded in the applications conversation.

### 2. An omitted state and an unprovided future input are different problems

Completed PC1 distinguishes sufficient present state, noisy observation,
recoverable missing current, and unknown future control. In the last case,
24 of 60 identical-past pairs have opposite outcomes under different future
inputs. More detail about that same past cannot choose the unprovided input.

**What this supplies:** a concrete reason not to prescribe the same refinement
for every unresolved answer. Correctly retaining ambiguity is different from
failing to find a useful state variable.
**Boundary:** synthetic known-equation development; no universal detector,
hardware performance claim, or molecular-mechanism validation.
Source: completed PC1 return, sections 1, 3, and 5.

### 3. Locally acceptable pieces versus the claimed whole

The Water interval case composes correct child summaries exactly. Its

**What this supplies:** a counterexample to confusing structural agreement,

## How the threads exchange work

Exchange short, versioned notes at meaningful result boundaries, not every
brainstorm. A transfer note needs only:

1. The proposed distinction or changed rule, in plain language.
2. Its precise assumptions and required observations/operations.
3. One worked example and one boundary, null, or failure.
4. What was actually proved, executed, or merely proposed, with source identity.
5. A concrete question for the receiving thread, if one exists.

A proposed definition may inform new exploration immediately, with exposure
recorded. An active scored experiment keeps its pinned protocol; revisions form
a new version. The two threads may disagree without rewriting each other's
results. A discovered defect that invalidates an active conclusion should be
communicated promptly rather than hidden until a routine milestone.
